## Logo Concept: "Nutrilite"
Core idea: a leaf that doubles as a lightbulb — nature + education in one mark.

## Visual description
- Icon: a stylized leaf whose veins form the filament of a lightbulb. Rounded, friendly geometry — no sharp corners. Reads instantly at small sizes (app icon, favicon).

- Wordmark: "Nutrilite" in a clean geometric sans-serif (e.g., Poppins or Montserrat SemiBold), lowercase with a capital N. The dot over the "i" replaced by a tiny leaf or seed.

- Color palette:
  - Primary green #3FA34D (fresh, natural)
  - Accent lime #A8D65A (energy, youth)
  - Warm yellow #F2C14E (the "light" of learning)
  - Neutral dark #1F3D2B for text

- Layout: horizontal lockup — icon left, wordmark right. Vertical stacked version for social avatars.

- Style: flat vector with a subtle two-tone gradient on the leaf; optional thin outline version for dark backgrounds.

## Symbolism
- Leaf → wholesome food, natural ingredients
- Lightbulb filament → knowledge, learning, "aha" moments
- Green + yellow → health + optimism

## Variations to produce
- Full color (primary)
- Monochrome dark green (print)
- White knockout (dark backgrounds)
- Icon-only (app/social avatar)
- Animated micro-version: leaf gently "glows" on load

## Tagline lockup
- Nutrilite — eat, learn, thrive
- Nutrilite — knowledge on your plate
